AUTOMATED FOLLOW-UP EMAILS: INSTALL

1. Unzip this folder, if you haven't already.
2. Double-click the installer for this computer:
     Windows:  Install (Windows).bat
               (If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".)
     Mac:      Install (Mac).command
               (If macOS says it can't be opened: right-click it, choose Open, then click Open.)
3. Answer the questions. When the browser opens, sign in with the
   business's Gmail account and click Allow.

That's it. The program is installed in a folder called "FollowupEmails" in
your home folder, and it runs by itself once a day.

DAILY USE
  Open "Follow-up Jobs" on the desktop. When a job is finished, set its
  status to "complete" and save, keeping the CSV format. The emails go out
  automatically.

TO STOP THE DAILY EMAILS
  Ask the person who installed it, or run:
     Windows:  %USERPROFILE%\FollowupEmails\.venv\Scripts\python.exe %USERPROFILE%\FollowupEmails\setup_wizard.py --uninstall
     Mac:      ~/FollowupEmails/.venv/bin/python ~/FollowupEmails/setup_wizard.py --uninstall
